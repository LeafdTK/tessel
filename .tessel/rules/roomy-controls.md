# Controls have room to breathe

Buttons, toggles, selectors and status lines get generous padding, and space from each other and from the edge of whatever holds them. Start with more room and trim it, never the other way round.

Good: a button with clear space around its label, rows of controls with an even gap, a card whose contents sit well inside its border.
Bad: a label touching its button's edge, two controls with no gap, a status line squeezed against a card's border, a row taller on one side because a control doesn't fit it.
